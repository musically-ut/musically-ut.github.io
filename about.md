> Notes and essays on machine learning, software, and the occasional book —
> written down before I forget.
{: .epigraph}

I'm Utkarsh Upadhyay — a machine-learning researcher who researches, codes,
blogs, and makes stuff. This is where the writing lives; the rest of me is over
at [musicallyut.xyz](https://musicallyut.xyz).

This site used to sit at <http://utkarshu.in> and
<http://musicallyut.blogspot.com>, then moved to Jekyll on GitHub Pages, where
it has stayed happily ever since.

## Colophon

Set in [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces) (display &
reading), [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) (interface &
metadata), and [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) (code).
Paper-and-ink palette with a single terracotta accent; light and dark.

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and served by
[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). Footnotes are written as plain
markdown and lifted into the margin as sidenotes on wide screens.[^sn]

[^sn]: Progressive enhancement — if JavaScript is off, or you're reading this in
a feed reader, the notes simply appear at the foot of the page instead.
